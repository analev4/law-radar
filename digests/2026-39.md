### France sets the bad-weather contribution (cotisation intempéries) at 0.68% or 0.13% of wages from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027.

**FR** · Arrêté · [Arrêté du 3 septembre 2026 relatif aux modalités de cotisation due par les entreprises au titre des arrêts de travail visé à l'article L.…](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000054854981) · published 18 September 2026 · confidence: high

**What changed:** Employers deduct a 96,168-euro allowance (abattement) from their wage base before the contribution rate applies. Employers get 80% of the heatwave stoppage reimbursement, and the fund can raise that share by 31 December 2026.

**Who:** The order hits French construction employers in the bad-weather scheme, across the 10-49 and 50-249 staff bands, through HR and finance payroll.

**When:** applies from 1 April 2026 (Article 3); deadline 31 December 2026 (Article 5)

**Penalty / money at stake:** Structural and public works firms pay 0.68% of wages after the 96,168-euro allowance, other firms pay 0.13%; the text sets no penalty amount.

**Scorecard (4/12):**
- Forcing mechanism: **yes**. Scheme employers pay 0.68% (structural and public works) or 0.13% of wages above a 96,168 euro allowance, from 1 April 2026.
- Findable: **yes**. SIRENE names construction companies by NAF code and staff band, but carries no flag for bad-weather scheme (régime intempéries) membership.
- Early: **no**. The rates cover a period that started on 1 April 2026, before this order was published, so employers get the figures after the period began.
- Gap evidence: **no**. No catalogue dataset records contribution payments, so no public record shows which firms have not applied the new rates.
- Crowding: **yes**. The order renews annual rates, a routine parameter update that every French construction payroll tool already applies.
