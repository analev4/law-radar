# Task: relevance filter

Decide whether one legal text could plausibly affect a company that matches the ICP below.

Relevant means the text creates, changes or removes an obligation, right, deadline, cost, aid or penalty for companies like these, or for the work of their buyers (for example HR, payroll or finance).

Not relevant means the text only concerns other sectors, the internal organisation of public bodies, named individuals, a single named company, or technical matters that do not reach these companies.

Return `relevant` (true or false) and `reason`: one sentence of at most 25 words, following the writing rules.
